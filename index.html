How long does a solar installation take?

Standard residential solar grid systems are typically completely mounted and structurally integrated within 2 to 3 working days, depending on roof complexity.

What happens to my power supply during rain or cloudy days?

Your systems automatically draw power seamless from your custom inverted backup battery storage banks rather than dropping out your household appliances.

Does the security framework function during power blackouts?

Yes. Absolute situational awareness requires zero down-time. All telemetry nodes, camera sensors, and perimeter matrix arrays run on isolated backup cells.

Can I track telemetry data remotely on mobile feeds?

Completely. Full live operational matrices are securely piped to your encrypted mobile applications with real-time push alert parameters active.
