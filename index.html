We Got Answers

Frequently asked questions

What is included in my private hire quote?

Our quotes are completely transparent and all-inclusive. Your rate covers the private vehicle, a dedicated professional chauffeur, fuel, all South African toll fees, and comprehensive passenger liability insurance. There are absolutely no hidden costs or surprise fees at the end of your journey.

Are your drivers professionally licensed and vetted?

Your safety is our absolute priority. Every single one of our drivers holds a valid South African Professional Driving Permit (PrDP), undergoes rigorous background checks, and possesses decades of accident-free driving experience across local routes.

Can we customize our itinerary or make unscheduled stops?

Absolutely. Unlike rigid tour buses, this is a premium private hire tailored entirely to your rhythm. If you spot a beautiful view, want to stop for a coffee, or need to adjust your route along the way, your driver is at your service.

How do airport pickups and transfers work?

We track your flight in real-time, so even if your landing is delayed, we will be there. Your driver will meet you directly at the arrivals hall holding a personalized name board, assist you with your luggage, and guide you straight to your waiting private vehicle.

What happens if our travel plans change?

We know that travel can be unpredictable. We offer flexible cancellation and rescheduling policies. Simply get in touch with us at least 24 hours before your scheduled pickup, and we will happily adjust your booking at no extra charge.

Which regions of South Africa do you operate in?

While we are based locally, our private hire services cover major hubs, airport transfers, scenic coastal routes, and long-distance travel between provinces. Let us know where you need to go, and we will map out the safest, most efficient route to get you there.
